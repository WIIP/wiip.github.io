What are the different standardization organizations found in Inventor's Content Center?

Last updated: Friday, September 5, 2025
InventorCAD
Acronym Full Name Country / Region
AFNOR Association Française de Normalisation 🇫🇷 France
ANSI American National Standards Institute 🇺🇸 United States
AS Standards Australia 🇦🇺 Australia
BSI British Standards Institution 🇬🇧 United Kingdom
CNS Chinese National Standards 🇹🇼 Taiwan
CSN Czech Standards (Česká státní norma) 🇨🇿 Czech Republic
DIN Deutsches Institut für Normung 🇩🇪 Germany
GB Guobiao Standards (National Standards of China) 🇨🇳 China
GOST Gosudarstvennyy Standart (State Standard) 🇷🇺 Russia (and former USSR)
IS Indian Standards (via BIS – Bureau of Indian Standards) 🇮🇳 India
ISO International Organization for Standardization 🌍 International
JIS Japanese Industrial Standards 🇯🇵 Japan
KS Korean Standards (via KATS – Korean Agency for Technology and Standards) 🇰🇷 South Korea
PN Polska Norma 🇵🇱 Poland
SFS Suomen Standardisoimisliitto (Finnish Standards Association) 🇫🇮 Finland
SS Swedish Standards (via SIS – Swedish Institute for Standards) 🇸🇪 Sweden
STN Slovenská Technická Norma 🇸🇰 Slovakia
UNI Ente Nazionale Italiano di Unificazione 🇮🇹 Italy

It should be noted that the European standardization organization, CEN (European Committee for Standardization), is not in the list, and therefore EN standards are not found.
