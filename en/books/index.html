My books

I write about the software I develop for every day. Since 2014 I have been the author of the Revit - Conception de bâtiment series published by Éditions ENI, reissued for every major release of the software. Before it came an AutoCAD 2000 workbook published by CampusPress. All of these books are written in French.

Writing a manual of several hundred pages means opening every dialog box, checking every behaviour and explaining why the software does what it does. It is the same work as custom development, carried through to the end: this is where the knowledge of Autodesk products my clients come looking for is built.

Revit - Conception de bâtiment

A reference manual on Revit, reissued for every major release of the software since 2014, published by Éditions ENI. It covers every function, from modelling a simple building to macro development, by way of complex family design: each command is detailed and each concept explained. The book is written in French.
